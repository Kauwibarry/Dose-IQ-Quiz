What we collect and why

If you complete the quiz and ask for the free guide, we collect:

  • your email address, so we can send you the Personalised Nutrition Guide;
  • your Fit Score (a lifestyle-habit score from the 10-question quiz), so we can understand which leads requested the guide.

The quiz answers themselves are used in your browser to calculate the Fit Score. We do not use the quiz to measure genetics, diagnose a condition, or provide medical advice.

We do not collect a phone number through this site. Please contact us only at info@dose-iq.com.

Legal basis

We process your email and Fit Score on the basis of your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You give that consent by ticking the checkbox on the quiz form before submitting. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing info@dose-iq.com.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade email lists. We do not share your details with third parties for their own marketing.

Processors

The quiz form is sent through Formspree, which processes the submission so we can receive your request. Formspree acts as a processor for this purpose. We do not currently run advertising pixels (including Meta Pixel) on this site.

How long we keep data

We keep your email and related Fit Score only as long as needed to send the guide, respond to your request, and handle deletion or other rights requests. If you ask us to delete your data, we will do so unless a limited legal obligation requires us to retain a record of the request.

Your GDPR rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and you may object to processing or withdraw consent. To do so, email info@dose-iq.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP) in Bulgaria, or with your local EU supervisory authority.

How to request deletion

Email info@dose-iq.com from the address you used on the form (or tell us which address to delete). We will delete your personal data from our lead records.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use a Meta Pixel or similar advertising tracker. The quiz may store a Fit Score locally in your browser after you successfully submit the form so you can download the guide in the same session.

Food supplements

Information on this site is educational. Food supplements should not replace a varied and balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle. We use authorised EU health claims only and do not claim to treat, cure, diagnose or prevent disease.
